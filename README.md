### 👨‍💻 About Me

<img align="right" alt="Dark Mode Coding Animation" width="340" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Anmol-Baranwal/Cool-GIFs-For-GitHub/main/assets/developer.gif" />

I am a **Software Engineer & R&D Executive** driven by a simple goal: *building resilient, high-throughput software where reliability is non-negotiable.*

Currently working as an **Executive – R&D at CG Power and Industrial Solutions Ltd.**, I engineer industrial-grade **WPF and SCADA/PLC software systems**. Before this, I spent nearly **5 years in FinTech at CoreCard Software**, architecting mission-critical **credit card authorization engines, ISO 8583 payment workflows, EMV/3DS security, and distributed .NET microservices**.

With a **B.Tech in Electronics & Communication** and an **MBA in Data Science & Business Analytics**, I bridge deep backend engineering with **Agentic AI, RAG pipelines, and full-stack product delivery**—while actively mentoring students and sharing practical engineering roadmaps on LinkedIn.

- 🔭 **Currently Engineering:** Industrial **WPF & SCADA/PLC** control interfaces at **CG Power R&D**
- 🚀 **Building in Public:** **FlexGCC** (Full-Stack .NET Tracker), AI-powered learning dashboards, & local RAG developer tools
- 🏅 **Certifications:** Microsoft Certified Full-Stack Developer | Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-900) | IBM RAG & Agentic AI
- 💡 **Core Strengths:** `.NET 8 / C#` • `FinTech Payment Engines` • `Distributed SQL & Kafka` • `Python & AI Automation`
- 📫 **Let's Connect:** **saurabh.nagaich.in@gmail.com** | 📱 **+91-9696780704**
